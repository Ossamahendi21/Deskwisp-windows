Questions

What's AI credit?

It's what your bots spend on the AI models they think and talk with. Every plan comes with some each month, and you can add more in the app when they've been busy.

What are computer hours?

The time your bots' computer is working for them. It sleeps when nothing's using it, and the hours start over each month.

Can I change or cancel my plan?

Yes, whenever you like, in the app under Account and usage.

Does it work on a Mac or on my phone?

The app is for Windows. From your phone, you can talk to your bots in Telegram, Discord, Slack or by email.
