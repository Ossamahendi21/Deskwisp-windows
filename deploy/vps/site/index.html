Questions

What is Deskwisp?

A Windows app that gives you a team of AI agents for your business operations. Each bot works on its own cloud computer, can have its own email address and a voice, remembers your business, and handles tasks like your inbox, invoices, sales follow-ups and reports.

How is it different from ChatGPT?

A chatbot answers in a chat window and leaves the work to you. Deskwisp bots do the work: they open apps and websites on their own computers, send and file things once you approve, keep working while you're away, and remember your customers.

Can I talk to my bots by voice?

Yes. Call any bot from the app: it answers in its own voice and does the work while you talk. You can also reach them in Telegram, Discord, Slack and by email.

Do the bots act without asking?

No. Looking things up happens straight away, but sending, creating and paying wait for your OK. You can watch every bot's computer live and take over at any time.

Is there a free plan?

Yes: $1 of AI credit and 10 computer hours every month, with as many bots as you like. Pro is $29 a month and Max $199 a month.

What's AI credit?

What your bots spend on the AI models they think and talk with. Every plan comes with some each month, and you can add more in the app.

What are computer hours?

The time your bots' cloud computer is working for them. It sleeps when nothing's using it, and the hours start over each month.

Does it work on a Mac or my phone?

The app is for Windows 10 and 11. From your phone, talk to your bots in Telegram, Discord, Slack or by email.
