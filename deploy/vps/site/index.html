FAQ

Questions, answered.

What is Deskwisp?

Deskwisp is a Windows app that gives your small business a team of AI agents, sometimes called AI employees. Each agent works on its own cloud computer, can have its own work email address and a voice, remembers your business, and handles back-office work such as the inbox, invoices and payment reminders, lead follow-ups and reports. You chat with it or call it, and it asks before it sends, buys or deletes anything.

What's the difference between an AI agent and an AI chatbot?

A chatbot answers in a chat window and leaves the work to you. An AI agent does the work: a Deskwisp agent opens the apps and websites it needs on its own computer, writes and files things, and reports back when it's done.

Is it safe to let an AI agent use my email and accounts?

Deskwisp agents look things up straight away, but sending, creating, buying and deleting wait for your OK. You can watch an agent's computer live and take over the mouse at any time.

What can a Deskwisp AI agent do?

Anything you'd do at a computer for your business: answer and sort email, chase unpaid invoices, follow up with leads, keep a sheet or CRM up to date, research suppliers and competitors, book calls and write the weekly report.

What's AI credit?

It's what your agents spend on the AI models they think and talk with. Every plan comes with some each month, and you can add more in the app when they've been busy.

What are computer hours?

The time your agents' cloud computer is working for them. It sleeps when nothing's using it, and the hours start over each month.

Can I change or cancel my plan?

Yes, whenever you like, in the app under Account and usage.

Does it work on a Mac or on my phone?

The app is for Windows 10 and 11. From your phone, you can talk to your agents in Telegram, Discord, Slack or by email.
